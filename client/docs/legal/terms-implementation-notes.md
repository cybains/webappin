# Terms rewrite — operational and contract boundaries

Internal record, 9 November 2026. Not a legal opinion or public notice.

- Replaces fictional accounts, checkout, subscriptions, AI/digital-access waiver, export tools and platform licences with the actual enquiry-to-written-service-agreement model.
- Preserves original policy at terms-before-2026-11-09.tsx.txt.
- Uses established support@sufoniq.com rather than the old unconfirmed legal@ address.
- Job seekers are not charged by Sufoniq, consistent with the public job-seeker page. No paid consumer product is introduced by the conditional statutory-rights section.
- The original SaaS indemnity and fee-based cap are not carried forward as approved recruitment terms. Employer contracts need an explicit, locally reviewed allocation of risk, liability caps/exclusions, scope, payment triggers, cancellation, approved costs and any replacement guarantee. Do not assume the website supplies those protections.
- Provide applicable general terms with each proposal before acceptance and preserve the accepted version. A published notice alone does not establish contractual incorporation.
- If acting as a processor, execute an Article 28 agreement appropriate to actual processing before it begins. No existing standard DPA is represented as executed. Determine actual roles first; recruitment activity can involve independent controllers.
- Before offering any paid consumer service, validate legality, pre-contract information, applicable withdrawal requirements, model form and separate early-start consent process. The public text is not an early-start consent and is not a checkout.
- Confirm country-specific recruitment permissions and worker-fee restrictions for engagements; the website does not establish licensing or authorise professional practice.
- Complaint workflow must support applicable Lithuanian 14-day written responses. The obsolete EU ODR platform reference was removed (closed July 2025).
- Use noindex, follow and exclude from XML sitemap consistently with the privacy page, while retaining public footer access.
