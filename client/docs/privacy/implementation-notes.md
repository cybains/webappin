# Privacy notice operational follow-through

Internal record, 9 November 2026. Not public copy or a compliance certification.

## Evidence

Owner confirms actual business enquiries and team handling, with a privacy contact rather than a claimed appointed DPO. Current public forms send contact and recruitment requirements through /api/contact and Resend to a business mailbox. That handler does not store submissions in a database. No public candidate accounts, scoring or document upload flow was found.

Domain MX records point to Zoho EU servers; that does not establish all account settings, contracts or processing locations. Resend states US data storage and a standard DPA incorporating EU SCCs. Existing company identity and address have been retained. Original policy is preserved in privacy-policy-before-2026-11-09.tsx.txt.

## Required operational checks

- Verify privacy@sufoniq.com is monitored, assign an owner and backup, and track requests and response deadlines. No test email was sent.
- Inventory actual production hosting, mailbox, CRM, document stores and backups. Record recipients, contracts, locations, access and transfer safeguards. Retain the applicable Resend account DPA; do not infer all processing is in Europe from Zoho DNS.
- Apply the notice's retention criteria to stored enquiries and exports. Establish a review schedule and relevant statutory periods; delete unnecessary candidate documents and isolate records required for accounting/disputes. No deletion automation was added by this rewrite.
- Verify team permissions, account security, forwarding rules, backup handling and incident procedures. No encryption-at-rest, zero-trust or audit claim has been made without evidence.
- Inspect the deployed site for actual cookies, storage and third-party requests, including hosting behaviour. Source review is not a production cookie audit. No claim that the site is cookie-free is made. Record observed essential cookies and obtain appropriate consent before optional tracking.
- Before collecting identity/health/background-check documents or making international candidate disclosures, establish necessity, legal basis (including any special-category condition), recipient, secure channel and lawful transfer mechanism. Consent to an introduction does not replace transfer safeguards.
- Check offline recruitment tools separately. The human-review statement is scoped to public enquiry forms, not every business tool.
- Confirm controller/processor roles in client agreements and whether operations trigger a DPO, UK representation or additional notices. Removing unsupported appointments does not determine whether one is legally required.
